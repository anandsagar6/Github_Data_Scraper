IMPORTANT CATEGORY 3 RULE:
A broad non-IT education keyword alone should not qualify a candidate.
Use it together with a meaningful IT course, certification, training,
project, or sufficiently strong IT evidence.

ACADEMIC-YEAR ORDINAL COVERAGE:
Every education/course/branch keyword now also has 1st year, 2nd year,
3rd year and 4th year forms, both with and without the word 'student'.
These academic-year combinations are intentionally not applied to job-role titles.

TARGET EXPERIENCE RANGE:
Freshers / 0 years plus experienced users with approximately 1 to 3 years experience.
Generic senior-only terms have been removed because they conflict with this target.

IMPORTANT DUPLICATE RULE:
Each exact keyword is listed only once in this arranged search library.
If a keyword logically belongs to more than one category, it is kept in
the higher-priority search group and should still be usable by the
classification logic for lower-priority categories.
